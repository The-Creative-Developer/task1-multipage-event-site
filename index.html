FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. Student passes are free and include lunch on both days. Professionals can buy a supporter pass to help cover costs.

Do I need to know how to code already?

No. The Day 1 workshops start from HTML and CSS. The hackathon has a beginner track with mentors.

Can I join the hackathon alone?

Yes. We run team matching on the morning of Day 2, so you can come solo and find a team there.

Seats are limited to 600
